# Music

Songs, albums, lyrics, credits and what's playing in Music or Spotify. Searches YouTube Music and the iTunes
store at once, so soundtracks, anime and game music and songs not on Apple Music in your country are found, and
every result carries its Apple Music link (here, or in another store) and its YouTube Music link.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB (time-synced) with YouTube Music as the fallback; credits from MusicBrainz.
No keys needed.
